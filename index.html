pronunciation Tips


  • Please note that SiSwati does not recognize the letter "r" so omit it completely when using the pronunciation guides.

  • Syllables are separated by a dash (-) and sounds are spaced by a plus (+).

  • For best results, pronounce each letter grouping separated by a + separately but quickly so they sound like one cohesive word with semi-standalone letters.

  • Some word translations provide more than 1 word for your searched English word, separated by a coma (,). Please note that only one pronunciation guide is provided for these and the context of the pronunciation may not apply to your situation.
